# Privacy notice and consent form (template)

> **Before using this with real patients:** this is a plain-language template, not legal advice. Fill in the [bracketed] parts and have it checked against the privacy and health-records law that applies where TPC operates (for example the Data Privacy Act in the Philippines, GDPR in the EU, HIPAA in the US). Print it, give the athlete a copy, and keep the signed copy in your records.

---

## Privacy notice for athletes — [TPC full name]

**Who we are:** [TPC name, address]. Contact for privacy questions: [name, phone, email].

**What we record about you.** Your name, age, sex, body weight, running goals and training history; health and injury screening answers; test results (running, strength, jumping, balance); video of you running and testing; your gym program; the sessions and weekly running you log; and notes from your clinician.

**Why.** To assess you, build and adjust your strength program, track your progress, and give you reports. We do not use your information for advertising and we do not sell it.

**How decisions are made.** Your physiotherapist makes every decision. The TPC system is a software tool that follows written rules and gives a second opinion on your test results. It does not use artificial intelligence services, and it never clears you medically. Your clinician can change or reject anything it suggests.

**Who can see it.** The TPC clinicians who treat you, and you (through your own login if you choose to use it). We do not share it with anyone else unless you ask us to, or the law requires it. [Add any referring doctor, coach or insurer you will share with, and only with the athlete's permission.]

**Where it is kept.** On [TPC's secured computer / secure hosting service in (country)], protected by passwords, with regular backups. [Add the encryption and backup details you have set up.]

**How long.** [For example: for X years after your last visit, as required by (rule)], then it is deleted.

**Your rights.** You may ask to see your information, correct it, receive a copy, or have it deleted (we may have to keep some records by law). You may withdraw consent at any time; this will not affect care already given. To do any of these contact [name and contact].

**Security problems.** If your information is ever lost or seen by someone who should not have seen it, we will tell you [and the authority, if the law requires] [within X days].
